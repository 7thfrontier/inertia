Ready to throw

Inertia lives in your menu bar.

Grant Accessibility access once:

  1. The first time you ⌃⌘-drag a window, macOS opens System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility.
  2. Turn on the switch next to Inertia.

Then hold ⌃⌘, drag any window, and flick it. Heavier windows glide farther. Try a big editor, then a little palette, and feel the difference.

Adjust the throw, momentum, and bounce anytime from the menu-bar icon.
