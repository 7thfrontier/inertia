Inertia

Throw your windows around.

Grab any window with ⌃⌘ + drag, give it a flick, and let go. It coasts across the screen with momentum, bouncing off the edges and keeping its size.

It has weight, too: a big window carries more inertia and glides farther; a little one snaps to a stop. Tune the feel from the menu bar.

One-time setup: the first time you use it, macOS asks you to grant Accessibility permission. Any app that moves windows needs it.
